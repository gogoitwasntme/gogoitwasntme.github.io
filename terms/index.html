In short: GOGO Publisher is a private tool for posting the IT WAS GOGO! cartoon episodes to our own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

1. What the app is

GOGO Publisher ("the app") is a desktop tool run by the creator of the animated children's series IT WAS GOGO! It uploads finished episodes to the series' own TikTok account (@itwasgogo) through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

2. Who may use it

The app is used only by the account owner. There are no public sign-ups, no other accounts and no paid features. Anyone who signs in agrees to these terms and to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Content

  • All videos posted through the app are original GOGO episodes owned by their creator.
  • The episodes are made with AI video tools, and the app marks each post as AI-generated content on TikTok.
  • Posts are family-friendly and follow TikTok's Community Guidelines. Duets and Stitches are turned off by default.
  • The account owner chooses each post's caption and privacy level before it is published.

4. Acceptable use

The app may not be used to post content the user does not own, to spam, to post on behalf of other people, or in any way that breaks TikTok's rules or the law.

5. Privacy

The app's handling of data is described in the GOGO Publisher Privacy Policy. In short, it stores only the TikTok sign-in token needed to post, on the owner's computer.
