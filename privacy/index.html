IT WAS GOGO! · GOGO Publisher

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short: GOGO Publisher is a private tool that posts our own GOGO cartoon episodes to our own TikTok account. It has no other users. It keeps only the TikTok sign-in token needed to post, on our own computer, and it never sells or shares data.

Who we are

GOGO Publisher is operated by the creator of the animated children's series IT WAS GOGO! (TikTok and Instagram: @itwasgogo). The app is used only by the channel's creator to upload finished episodes to the channel's own TikTok account.

What information the app uses

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives, through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  • an access token and a refresh token, used only to post videos to that account;
  • basic account details (the account's username and display name, its internal TikTok ID, and the posting options TikTok allows, such as privacy levels and maximum video length).

The app does not read followers, messages, contacts, location, viewing history or any other TikTok user's data. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How it is used

This information is used only to upload our own videos, set their caption, cover, privacy level and AI-generated label, and confirm that a post finished. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored and for how long

Tokens are stored in a local file on the operator's own computer and are not sent to any server other than TikTok's. Access tokens expire after 24 hours, and refresh tokens expire after 365 days. The file is deleted when the app is no longer used.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok, as required to publish videos through its API.

Children

Our videos are made for families, but the app itself is used only by the adult account owner. The app collects no information from children or from viewers of our videos.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. When access is removed, the stored tokens stop working, and we delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. For questions, send a message to @itwasgogo on TikTok or Instagram.
