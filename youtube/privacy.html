Privacy Policy – GOGO Uploader

Last updated: November 11, 2026

GOGO Uploader ("the App") is a private, single-user tool operated by Yair Levy ("we", "us") to publish the animated children's series IT WAS GOGO! to our own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, stored and deleted.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the channel it is authorized for, and to read the view, like and comment counts of that channel's own videos. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

2. Who uses the App

The App is not offered to the public. Its only user is the owner of the IT WAS GOGO! channel. It has no sign-up, no public accounts, and it does not collect any information from viewers of our videos, including children.

3. Data the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0), the App requests these scopes:

  • youtube.upload – to upload the series' videos with their title, description, tags, privacy setting and "made for kids" designation.
  • youtube.readonly – to read the signed-in channel's ID, name and public totals (subscriber, video and view counts), to confirm that uploads go to the correct channel, and to read the list of the channel's own uploads with each video's title, privacy status and view, like and comment counts.

The App does not read the text of comments, the identity of subscribers or viewers, YouTube Analytics reports, payment information, or any other channel's data.

4. How data is used

Data is used only to upload our own videos, to verify the destination channel, and to show the channel owner how the series' own videos are performing. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to build profiles of anyone.

5. Storage

The App runs locally on the channel owner's computer. The Google OAuth access/refresh token is stored only in a local file on that computer, with access restricted to the owner's user account. The App keeps a local record of the YouTube video IDs it uploaded. When the owner runs a statistics check, the App saves a dated snapshot of the counts listed above in a local file and the owner may view them in a private dashboard that only the owner can open. No data is stored on any server operated by us, and API data is not cached beyond what is described here.

6. Deleting data and revoking access

  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Deleting the local token file immediately removes the App's stored credentials. After access is revoked or the App stops being used, we delete stored tokens and all stored API data within 7 days.
  • To request deletion of any data related to the App, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

Our videos are made for children and are designated "made for kids" on YouTube. The App itself is used only by an adult channel owner and collects no personal information from children.

8. Changes

We will update this page if the App's data practices change, and update the date above.
